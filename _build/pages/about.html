The short version

Commercially minded, internationally focused, and still curious.

I’m an Account Executive in international logistics at OEC Group, based in Houston. My work centers on developing international accounts across Latin America and global trade lanes, with a growing specialization in bulk liquids.

I didn’t start in logistics. I started in sales, then led sales teams, then ran a high-volume operation with more than 60 employees and full responsibility for its financial performance. That gave me something I still lean on every day: an understanding of what it means to own a number, and what a customer needs before they’ll trust you with their business.

Moving into logistics at HOYER Group put that foundation to work in a much more technical world: quoting, order execution, pricing, and keeping key accounts running when something went sideways. Now at OEC Group, I’m building international relationships, mostly in Latin America, and learning the bulk liquid side of the industry in depth.

I’m bilingual in English and Spanish, I’m finishing a BBA in Supply Chain Management, and I like learning how my customers’ businesses actually work. That last part is usually where the best logistics solutions come from.
